Entry for the [make.fun competition "One-Liner Laughs"](https://make.fun/c/u-coral-404-one-liner-laughs-aa872d41): one funny joke, a single line of at most 200 characters.

`main.ts` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I asked my GPS for the fastest route home and it said, 'Honestly? Therapy.'

Run with Node 22+: `node main.ts`

The line is 75 characters — well inside the 200-character gate — and was picked
from 8 fresh candidates (see `CANDIDATES-one-liner-laughs-01.md` in the watch
workspace) against the judge's brief: a real setup + payoff, a laugh, a surprise
twist on the last word, a fresh GPS-meets-therapy angle, and no wasted words.
`main.ts` self-checks the 1–200 character gate and fails loudly rather than
writing an entry that would fail it.
